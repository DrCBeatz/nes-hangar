Original composition: At Doom's Gate, Bobby Prince, Doom (1993).
New NES arrangement supplied by Dr C Beatz.

The contributor reports entering every note from scratch, listening to the
original recording and consulting sheet music and rasteri's version, with
most harmonies transcribed by ear. This records the supplied provenance;
it does not make a determination about legal independence or distribution.
No project license has been changed as part of this local integration.

Native FamiStudio tempo, volume track and vibrato; four standard APU channels.
DPCM is disabled, the sample table is empty, and no sample binary is linked.
The export contains 2,309 assembled bytes. Its native tempo envelope is
$03,$05,$80. The music_data_untitled symbol is the author's export symbol.

Earlier reference arrangements and their notices are retained together in
local research history, outside this published package. They are not bundled
or compiled into the current ROM. See docs/PROVENANCE.md for the research
history and consulted references.
